# Privacy

Tally Bar works offline. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs, or network entitlements.

Session names and timestamps stay in the app's local sandbox. CSV export writes finished sessions to the location you select. Exported files can contain client names or other information you put into session titles; you control where those files go.

Delete individual sessions from History. To remove all data, quit Tally Bar and remove its `sessions.json` file at the location documented in the README. Deleting the app alone does not necessarily delete its sandbox or exported files. Backups and cloud-synced export folders follow your system settings.
